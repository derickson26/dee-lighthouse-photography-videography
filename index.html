About Dee

Twelve years of chasing the same light, on a different coastline every weekend.

I started out photographing the lighthouse keepers and fishing crews around Port Asterly — long days spent waiting for a single usable hour of light taught me to work fast and stay quiet. That habit followed me into weddings.

I don't direct much. I watch for the moment the light does something interesting to a face, and I'm already there when it happens.

"The best photograph of your wedding is usually the one nobody asked me to take."
