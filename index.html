Quiet observation, honest moments.

Dee Lighthouse Photography & Videography was built around one idea: the best wedding coverage doesn't interrupt the day, it disappears into it. We work in natural light wherever we can find it, and we stay close enough to catch the moments that happen only once.

Every couple gets both a photographer's eye and a filmmaker's patience — so the day is preserved as stills to hold in your hands, and as a film to watch again on your tenth anniversary.

I started shooting weddings after filming my sister's, and realized the two crafts belong together — a photograph holds a moment still, and a film lets it move again. That's the whole idea behind this studio.

These days I split my time between weddings on the coast and elopements further afield, usually with a second shooter and a small film crew alongside me. I care more about how a day felt than how it looked on a shot list.
