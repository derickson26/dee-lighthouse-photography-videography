About my photography business

Welcome to my photography business, a small part-time photography service based in Port Vila, Vanuatu. I am passionate about capturing special moments and creating lasting memories through photography.

I provide photography services for a variety of occasions, including birthdays, weddings, graduations, dinner functions, outdoor portraits, and other special events held in and around Port Vila.

As this is a part-time business, I am available for photography bookings Monday to Friday after my regular working hours, from 5:00 PM onwards, and I am also available throughout weekends, depending on my schedule.

Photography Rate
My photography service is 5,000 VT per hour, regardless of the type of event or photoshoot.

Photo Delivery
After your photoshoot, your photos can be delivered in several convenient ways:

  • 📱 Sent directly to you through WhatsApp
  • 📸 Selected photos may be posted on our official Facebook page
  • 💾 Photos can be transferred to a hard drive or USB flash drive for the customer

My goal is to provide an affordable and flexible photography service while capturing the important moments that you and your family can look back on and enjoy for years to come.

For bookings and enquiries, please feel free to contact me and discuss your photography needs and preferred date and time.
